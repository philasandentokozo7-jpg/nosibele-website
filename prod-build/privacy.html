What we collect

  • Enquiry and quote details you choose to send us — such as your name, contact details and what you would like embroidered or printed.
  • Website usage data — only if you accept analytics cookies (see below). This is aggregated information such as which pages were visited, not your identity.

We do not ask for or store payment card details on this website.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quotation.
  • To produce and deliver an order you have placed with us.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the website is used so we can improve it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Cookies

A cookie is a small file stored by your browser. This site uses two kinds:

  • Essential — needed for the site to work and to remember your cookie choice. These are always active and do not track you.
  • Analytics (optional) — Google Analytics, used only to measure how the site is used. These load only if you press "Accept" on the cookie notice. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and no analytics script is loaded. Where analytics is enabled, we ask Google to anonymise your IP address.

Change your mind at any time. Clearing your choice below will make the cookie notice appear again so you can accept or decline.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and order information for as long as we need it to serve you and to meet our legal and accounting obligations, after which it is deleted.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. You may also object to how we use it, and withdraw consent for analytics cookies at any time using the button above. To make any of these requests, email info@nosibeleembroidery.co.za.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Other websites

Our pages link to services such as WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok and Google Maps. Once you follow those links you are on their platforms, and their own privacy policies apply.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
