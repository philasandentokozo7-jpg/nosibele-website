Our Story

Threaded with intention, worn with pride.

Nosibele Design & Embroidery is a South African studio for bespoke embroidery, sublimation, DTF printing and custom apparel.

Who we are

A studio built on detail.

We bring brands, teams and personal stories to life in thread and print. What started as a love of hand-finished detail has grown into a full custom-apparel house — embroidery, sublimation, DTF printing and made-to-order garments.

From corporate uniforms and workwear to varsity regalia, ceremonial pieces and one-off gifts, our promise stays the same: premium materials, careful finishing, and apparel you’ll be proud to wear.
